Rules of Engagement

cat /etc/rules — read before you hack. Ignorance is not a defense.

⚔️ COMPETITION RULES

  • The use of generative AI tools is strictly prohibited during both the qualifiers and finals.
  • Sharing flags, solutions or answers between teams is strictly prohibited.
  • Participants must work only with their registered team members.
  • Account sharing between teams is not allowed.

🖥️ CHALLENGE & INFRASTRUCTURE RULES

  • Attacking the CTF platform, scoreboard, organisers' infrastructure or other teams is strictly prohibited.
  • Denial-of-service (DoS/DDoS) attacks are not allowed.
  • Participants may only attack systems explicitly designated as challenge targets.
  • Exploiting the scoring system or manipulating flag submissions is prohibited.
  • Sharing challenge solutions or write-ups publicly before the competition concludes is not allowed.

🛡️ ENFORCEMENT & ADMINISTRATION

  • Any violation may result in warnings, point deductions or disqualification.
  • The organisers reserve the right to investigate suspicious activity and rule violations.
  • Technical issues or challenge questions go through the official Discord support channels.
  • Official announcements and clarifications will be communicated through the designated channels.

🎤 QUALIFICATION INTERVIEWS

Following the online qualifiers, teams that successfully qualify for the finals will be required to undergo a short interview.

This is to verify that participants understand the challenges they solved and can explain their solutions, methodologies and approaches.

Further details regarding the event — especially the finals — will be released later.
